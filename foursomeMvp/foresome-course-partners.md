## Status legend

- **Not contacted** — on the list, no outreach yet
- **Reached out** — initial contact made, awaiting response
- **In talks** — active conversation, nothing committed
- **Agreed** — confirmed partnership; record exactly what was promised
- **Declined** — passed; note why if known

---

## Rule

Only **Agreed** courses with a written/confirmed commitment should be treated as real partners in any pitch, landing page, or investor material. "In talks" is not a partner yet.
